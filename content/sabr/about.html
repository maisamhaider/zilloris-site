Quit one habit. Build another. One line a day.

Sabr gives every habit a single brush line on paper, and you draw one segment of it a day. The line is the whole record: how long you have kept at it, and where you have not.

A line, not a number

Each habit — the one you are quitting and the one you are building — is a brush line drawn on paper. One day is one segment. For a habit you are building, you draw the day's segment yourself; for one you are quitting, the line draws itself through the day while the pen stays down.

A missed day is a pen lift

Miss a day and the line does not break. It shows a saffron pen lift: a dot where the brush came off the paper, and then the line carries on from where it is. Nothing before it is undone.

When it gets hard, hold

For a habit you are quitting, press and hold when a craving comes. The ink pools while you breathe with it, and a craving held all the way through leaves a small dot on the line.

It stays on your phone

No account, no sign-up and nothing to sync. Your line names, your daily amounts, your pen lifts, your holds and your settings are stored on your phone and nowhere else. The only copy that leaves is one you make yourself: Settings offers an export of your page as a CSV file, through Android's own share sheet, to wherever you send it. The details are in the privacy policy.

On your home screen

An optional widget shows today's lines, and one press on the small one goes straight into Hold.

Free

Sabr is free. There are no ads and nothing to buy inside it.
