Using the app

Sabr is a habit tracker for Android: one habit you are quitting, one you are building, each drawn as a brush line with a segment for every day. It is free, it has no account, and there is nothing to buy inside it. Installing it is the whole of it — there is nothing to register and nothing to cancel.

Sabr is not directed at children under 13.

Your record is yours

What you put into Sabr belongs to you. The names you give your lines, the amounts you record, the days you lifted the pen and the days you held — all of it is yours. We claim no ownership of it, and we never receive it: there is no account, no sync and no server of ours for it to be sent to.

Because we never receive it, we cannot read it, cannot recover it, and have nothing to hand to anyone else.

Your copies are yours to keep

Sabr keeps your record in one place: your phone. Settings offers an export of your page as a CSV file, through Android's own share sheet, to a place you choose. If you send it into a cloud folder or another app, the file is then subject to that service's terms and outside anything this app can control.

Deleting the app deletes the page, immediately and permanently, and a copy you exported earlier is not affected — it stays where you put it.

The app is provided as it is. It is a habit tracker on a phone: it can be uninstalled, a device can be lost, and data kept in one place can be gone. Export anything you would be sorry to lose. To the extent the law allows, the app and its author are not liable for lost data or lost time.

Nothing here removes rights you have as a consumer that cannot be excluded under the law where you live.
