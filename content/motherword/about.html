Your mother tongue first, for every language.

Motherword gives you back the language of your home: the words you half know, the letters of its script you never met, and your elders' voices. A few words a day, in your family's language, and nothing is ever marked wrong.

Take a word back

Five words a day, each with how it sounds, what it means and one everyday sentence. Beside each one sits the borrowed word people say instead. Both are right; you keep the one you want to use. A kept word becomes a painted tile in your courtyard, and comes back for a look after a day, three days, a week, two weeks and a month.

Letters you don't know yet

If you already read your country's script, most of your home script is already yours. The app shows only the letters that differ, with the sound each makes and a word that uses it. Where the north and the south say a letter differently, you choose your family's way.

Ask your elders

Each week brings one question to ask an elder: what your mother called you when you were small, what the first rain of the season is called. Record the answer in the app, or send the question on WhatsApp and share the voice note back into Motherword. Elders never need the app.

Family word book

Every voice you keep, by person, on your phone. Play it, share it, or let it go. Recording and keeping is free, with nothing to unlock.

Kept on your phone

No account, no sign-up and nothing to sync. Your kept words, your recordings, the names you give the people in them and your settings are stored on your phone, not on our servers. A recording leaves it only when you share it yourself. If your phone's own backup is switched on, Android includes Motherword in it. The details are in the privacy policy.

Free to download

Motherword is free, with no ads and nothing to buy inside it.
