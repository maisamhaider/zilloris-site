Kasrat — Delete your account

App: Kasrat (com.zilloris.kasrat), by Zilloris
Updated: 15 November 2026
Contact: support@zilloris.com

Kasrat works without an account. It asks you to sign in only when you share with a club's machine list or add a club. If you signed in, you can delete that account at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Kasrat, then Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. If your account has a password, type it once more: this proves it is you. It is done at once.

Without the app

Write to support@zilloris.com from the email address you signed in with, and say "Delete my Kasrat account". We answer to that address to be sure it is you, and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: the sign-in (your email address, and your password if you chose one, kept for us by Google's Firebase Authentication) and the account number on our server.
  • What you shared with clubs: your answers about machines, where machines are, and gym facts are taken back from the lists.
  • The link between you and anything that stays (see below).

What is kept

  • Reports you sent about a mistake or a rule broken stay, because the law asks us to keep a record of them, but they no longer point to you.
  • A club list's history keeps the line that a change was made, marked "deleted" instead of your member code.
  • Crash reports already sent to Firebase Crashlytics are deleted on Google's standard schedule; they hold no account.

What was never ours

Your plan, your workouts, your weights and your notes live only on your phone. Deleting the account does not touch them, and signing out does not either. To remove them, delete the app's data or uninstall Kasrat.

Accounts nobody uses

An account that has not been used for 13 months is deleted by itself.
