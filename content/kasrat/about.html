Train on the machines your gym really has.

Kasrat starts from your gym, not from a generic workout. Tick the machines your gym has, once, and get a month plan that uses only them, with a second choice for every exercise when a machine is busy.

Tick your gym's machines

Main machines and rarer ones, grouped by body area. A cable station or a Smith machine counts as everything it can do. Training at two gyms? Tick both, and each day says where it works best.

A plan that always has a second choice

Every exercise comes with a first choice, a second choice and stand-ins at your gym, so a busy machine never stops the workout. A weekly check makes sure no muscle group is left out.

See what each machine works

Each machine has a picture of the body on it, with a switch from skin to the muscles at work, how to use it, and the alternatives your gym has.

One tap at the machine

Tick each exercise as you finish it. Kasrat adds a rep when a session goes well, and asks before you go heavier, in the machine's own weight steps. It can remind you on your training days.

Kept on your phone

No account and no sign-up. Your plan, your log and your weights stay on your phone. The details are in the privacy policy.

Free to download

Kasrat is free, with no ads and nothing to buy inside it. In English and French.
