Using the app

One Page is a reading habit tracker. It is free, it has no account, and there is nothing to buy inside it.

Your books are yours

The app reads EPUB files you already own. You are responsible for having the right to the files you import. One Page does not supply books, does not host them, and provides no way to pass an imported book on to anyone else.

Shared quotations are capped at roughly a page for the same reason.

Your streak

The streak is a record of what you did, kept on your device. It is not a score, it is not compared with anyone, and it is not worth anything to anyone but you. The occasional recognition check exists only so the record stays true — it never ends a streak.

The app is provided as it is. It is a habit tracker on a phone: it can be uninstalled, a device can be lost, and data kept in one place can be gone. Export anything you would be sorry to lose. To the extent the law allows, the app and its author are not liable for lost data or lost time.
