A safe place for your family's papers.

Passports, ID cards, driving licences, car papers, insurance. Each person in the family gets a drawer, each paper keeps its end date (or "It doesn't run out"), and Arkiv reminds you before anything runs out, so a passport is less likely to surprise you just before a trip.

Scan it once

Scan a paper and the page comes out straight, cropped and clean, like a scanner made it. Add up to 8 pages to a paper: both sides of a card, or the passport pages you need. On a phone without Google's scanner, Arkiv keeps one photo for each paper. Arkiv reads the end date and the number for you; you check them and save.

Reminded in time

If you allow Arkiv's notifications, your phone tells you two months, one month and one week before a paper runs out. You can turn off the ones you don't want, but one always stays on. The reminder dates can go into your calendar too.

Share it, print it

Share any paper as a PDF in one tap, or print it. When a card is scanned right to its edges, it prints at its real size on A4 paper, with the front and back together on one sheet. Turn photos into one PDF, join PDFs, take pages out, or lock a PDF with a password.

Turn on a backup in your own Google Drive

Keep a locked backup in your Google Drive, or anywhere you choose, renewed shortly after every change. On a new phone, open it with your password: your papers, their pages and your reminder days come back. If you used "Also put them in my calendar", switch it on again in Settings.

Locked on this phone

Your papers are kept encrypted on your phone and never uploaded by the app, with no account and no server of ours — we could never see them. Arkiv asks for your fingerprint or your phone's screen lock (PIN, pattern or password) before it opens, and face on phones that allow it. This is on from the start if your phone has a screen lock, and you can turn it off in Settings. The details are in the privacy policy.

Free
