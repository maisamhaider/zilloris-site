Using the app

Arkiv keeps your household's papers on your phone — passports, ID cards, licences, car papers, insurance — reminds you before they run out, and makes PDFs of them to share or print. It is free to download, has no ads and has nothing to buy. There is no account: installing it is the whole of it.

Arkiv is not directed at children under 13.

Your papers are yours

What you put into Arkiv belongs to you, and you are responsible for what you add and who you share it with. We claim no ownership of it, and we never receive it: Arkiv cannot connect to the internet, and we have no server for it to be sent to. Because we never receive it, we cannot read it, recover it or hand it to anyone.

Reminders are a help, not a guarantee

Phones can delay or stop an app's reminders, and a date read from a paper can be read wrong. Check the dates Arkiv shows against the papers themselves. Renewing on time stays your responsibility.

Backups are yours to keep

A backup file opens only with the password you chose for it. We cannot recover a lost password, and we cannot bring back a lost phone's papers without a backup. When you keep a backup in your Google Drive or anywhere else, that place's own terms apply to the file.

Services we do not run

Google Play distributes the app and its updates, under Google Play's terms. Google's document scanner, part of Google Play services, runs the scanning screen. Google Drive, or whichever app holds your backup, stores it under its own terms. GitHub Pages publishes these pages at zilloris.com; they open in your browser.
