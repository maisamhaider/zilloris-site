Sabr

In development

Quit one habit. Build another. One line a day.

Sabr gives every habit a single brush line on paper, and you draw one segment of it a day. The line is the whole record: how long you have kept at it, and where you have not.

A line, not a number

Each habit — the one you are quitting and the one you are building — is a brush line drawn on paper. One day is one segment. You mark the day, the line grows, and that is the whole interface.

A missed day is a pen lift

Miss a day and the line does not break. It shows a saffron pen lift: a dot where the brush came off the paper, and then the line carries on. Nothing is reset and nothing is taken away.

Take a hold on purpose

Days you know you cannot draw can be put on hold, so the record says what actually happened rather than punishing you for it.

It stays on your phone

No account, no sign-up and nothing to sync. Your line names, your daily amounts, your pen lifts, your holds and your settings are stored on your phone and nowhere else. The only copy that leaves is one you make yourself: Settings offers an export of your page as a CSV file, through Android's own share sheet, to wherever you send it. The details are in the privacy policy.

Free

Sabr is free. There are no ads and nothing to buy inside it.
