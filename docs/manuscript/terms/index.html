4your backups are your responsibility

This is the most important practical thing in this document.

Manuscript keeps your writing in one place: your phone. There is no account, no cloud sync and no copy held by us. Android’s automatic backup is switched off for this app, so a factory reset, a lost or broken phone, or a move to a new phone will not bring your pages with it.

If the phone is gone and you have not written a copy out, the writing is gone. Not recoverable by you, and not recoverable by us — because there is no copy anywhere else to recover it from. That is the cost of an app with no server, and it is yours to plan around.

Making a copy takes two taps. In the app, go to “you” → “a copy of everything”. You can write out either of two files:

  • “the words” — a plain .txt file of the draft currently on the desk. Readable in any text editor. It is words only; it cannot be put back into the app, and it does not include drafts sitting in the drawer.
  • “everything, to put back” — a .manuscript file. This is the full copy: every draft, every page, the days, the streak, the keep, the red pen, the outline and your settings. It is the only file the app can restore from.

Both are written through your phone’s own file picker, to the place you choose. The app never writes a file anywhere you have not pointed at, and never uploads one.

A few things to know about those files:

  • A .manuscript file is plain, unencrypted text with your pages inside it. Whoever holds the file can read the book. Keep it somewhere you trust. If you save it into a cloud folder, it is in that cloud, under that provider’s terms, and outside anything this app can control.
  • Putting a copy back replaces what is on the phone — the pages, the days, the red pen, all of it. There is no undo, and if a restore fails part-way, what was on the phone may already be gone. Write a copy of what is there first.
  • “delete everything” does exactly that, immediately and permanently. It cannot be undone, and files you exported earlier are not affected — they stay where you put them.

We recommend writing a .manuscript copy regularly and keeping it somewhere that is not this phone.
