Note Bolt

In testing on Google Play

Write it down. It saves itself.

Note Bolt is a fast, simple notepad for everything you want to remember: ideas, shopping lists, plans, meeting notes and to-dos. Open it, tap plus, and start typing. There is no save button.

Your notes stay on your phone

No account and no sign-up. Notes are stored on your device, not on our servers, and the app works fully offline.

Write the way you think

Bold, italic, underline and strikethrough. Headings, bullet and numbered lists, and checklists that show your progress right on the card.

Organise at a glance

Nine colours to sort notes by sight. Pin what matters, bookmark what you come back to, and archive what you're done with. Deleted notes wait in Trash for 30 days, so nothing is lost by accident.

Lock and remind

Lock a note with a PIN or a pattern, and open it with your fingerprint or face on supported phones. Set a reminder on any note: in an hour, tomorrow morning, or at a time you choose.

Share and back up

Share a note as text, as an image, or as a PDF. Export all your notes to a backup file, and import them again on a new phone.
