Arkiv

In development

A safe place for your family's papers.

Passports, ID cards, driving licences, car papers, insurance. Each person in the family gets a drawer, each paper gets its date, and Arkiv reminds you before anything runs out — so a passport never surprises you two weeks before a trip.

Scan it once

Scan a paper and the page comes out straight, cropped and clean, like a scanner made it. Add both sides of a card or every page of a passport. Arkiv reads the end date and the number for you; you check them and save.

Reminded in time

Two months, one month and one week before a paper runs out, your phone tells you. The reminders can go into your calendar too.

Share it, print it

Share any paper as a PDF in one tap, or print it. ID cards print at their real size, front and back on one page. Turn photos into one PDF, join PDFs, take pages out, or lock a PDF with a password.

Backed up in your own Google Drive

Keep a locked backup in your Google Drive, or anywhere you choose, renewed after every change. On a new phone, open it with your password and everything comes back.

Locked on this phone

Arkiv cannot connect to the internet. Your papers are encrypted on your phone, with no account and no server of ours — we could never see them. Lock the app with your fingerprint or your phone's PIN. The details are in the privacy policy.

Free

Arkiv is free to download, with no ads and nothing to buy.
