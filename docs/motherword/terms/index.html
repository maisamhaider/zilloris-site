Using the app

Motherword is an Android app for taking your mother tongue back: a few words a day in the language of your family, the letters of its script you have not met, and your elders' voices, recorded and kept on your phone. It is free to download, it has no ads and nothing to buy inside it, and it has no account. Installing it is the whole of it — there is nothing to register and nothing to cancel.

Motherword is not directed at children under 13.

Your words and your recordings are yours

What you put into Motherword belongs to you: the words you keep, the recordings you make or bring in, and the names you give the people in them. We claim no ownership of any of it, and we never receive it: there is no account, no sync and no server of ours for it to be sent to. Because we never receive it, we cannot read it, cannot recover it, and have nothing to hand to anyone else.

Recording other people

The recordings in the family book are made by you, of people you know. You are responsible for asking them first, and for where you send a recording if you share one out of the app. The app makes no recording on its own and shares nothing on its own.

The language packs

The words, sentences, letters and questions in a language pack are written and reviewed by the people named in the pack. A pack marked as a draft has not finished its review, and the app says so on the card. They are offered to help, not as a dictionary: the way your family says a word is right, and the app never marks a word wrong.

Your copies are yours to keep

Motherword keeps everything in one place: your phone. The family book can hand a recording to Android's share sheet, to a place you choose; once it is there, it is subject to that service's terms and outside anything this app can control. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept, immediately and permanently, and a recording you shared out earlier is not affected.

No warranty

The app is provided as it is. It is a small app on a phone: it can be uninstalled, a device can be lost, and data kept in one place can be gone. Share out anything you would be sorry to lose. To the extent the law allows, the app and its author are not liable for lost data or lost time.
