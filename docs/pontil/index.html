Pontil

In development — get Pontil

Press to breathe out. Let go to breathe in. One minute, and a glass to keep.

Pontil is a breathing timer. Press and hold the glass to breathe out, and it grows; let go, and you breathe in. Six breaths, about a minute, and the glass cools and goes on your shelf.

The glass keeps the shape of your breathing

Every breath out lays one band of the wall. A full breath lays a full band, and a short one lays a narrower band — and the glass keeps it. There is no score and nothing to beat. A short breath is said once, kindly, and then it is just part of the glass.

One pattern, for now

This version keeps time for one pattern: five and a half seconds out, five and a half seconds in, six times. It is the pace most people settle into, and the one to start with.

Your shelf

Every glass you make goes on the shelf, by day. Open one to see each breath. Break one if you want it gone. A day you did not breathe is simply an empty place, not a mark against you.

With your eyes shut

A small vibration marks the end of each count, so you can breathe without watching the screen. It can be turned off in Settings.

Kept on your phone

No account, no sign-up and nothing to sync. There is no microphone and nothing is recorded: the press of your finger is the breath. Your shelf and your settings are stored on your phone, not on our servers. If your phone's own backup is switched on, Android includes Pontil in it. The details are in the privacy policy.

Free to download
