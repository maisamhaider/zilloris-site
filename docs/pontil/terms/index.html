Using the app

Pontil is a breathing timer for Android. You press to breathe out and let go to breathe in, and each session makes a small drawn glass that keeps the shape of your breathing. It is free to download and it has no account. Installing it is the whole of it — there is nothing to register and nothing to cancel.

Pontil is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

Pontil is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. The breathing pattern it keeps time for is a gentle, widely used one, but it is still exercise. If it makes you dizzy, faint or unwell, stop. Do not use it while driving, swimming or doing anything that needs your full attention. If you have a heart or breathing condition, or are pregnant, ask a doctor before using breathing exercises.

Your shelf is yours

What you make in Pontil belongs to you. The glasses on your shelf and the breaths in them are yours. We claim no ownership of them, and we never receive them: there is no account, no sync and no server of ours for them to be sent to.

Because we never receive them, we cannot read them, cannot recover them, and have nothing to hand to anyone else.

One place
