Privacy Policy

App: Pontil (com.zilloris.pontil)
Updated: 13 October 2026
Contact: support@zilloris.com

The short version

Your breathing stays on your phone. Every glass on the shelf — when you made it, how many breaths went into it, and how long each one was — is stored on your device and is never uploaded by the app. There is no account, no sign-in and no sync.

The app has no microphone permission and never listens. The press of your finger is the breath; nothing is recorded.

The app sends two narrow things to Google Firebase: a crash report if the app breaks, so the fault can be fixed, and a request for the addresses of this policy and the terms, which it then downloads from zilloris.com so what you read is the text in force today. Neither carries anything about your breathing. There is no analytics, no advertising and no advertising identifier, and nothing is sold or used to profile you.

Android's own backup, described below, can copy this app's data into your own Google Drive if you have device backup switched on.

What the app stores, and where

The shelf — each glass, the time it was made, the number of breaths in it, the length of each breath out, and whether it was finished or stopped early — and your settings are stored in the app's own private storage on your device.

There is no account to make, nothing to sign in to, and no copy of any of it held by us. We have no server for it to be held on.

What leaves the device

Only the two things described in the next section. Nothing else. Your shelf, your breaths and your settings are never transmitted by the app, and the app offers no export of them.

Crash reports and configuration

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics). If the app crashes, a report of what the code was doing at that moment is sent so the fault can be found and fixed: the technical trace, the app version, your device model and Android version, whether the phone was low on memory or storage, and a random installation identifier. It never contains anything from your shelf.

Configuration (Firebase Remote Config). To know where this policy and the terms live, the app asks Firebase for two addresses. That request carries the same random installation identifier and the standard technical details such a request carries: the app version, your device model, your language, your country and your network address. What comes back is two addresses and nothing else.

The two documents themselves are published at zilloris.com, on GitHub Pages, a GitHub service. When you open either one inside the app, the app downloads that page. It is an ordinary web request, carrying the page's address and — like any web request — your network address. Nothing of yours goes with it.

Firebase is run for us by Google LLC, which processes this data on our behalf under Google's privacy policy. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for a limited period and deleted on Google's standard schedule. There is no account, so none of it can be looked up by who you are.

The app contains no analytics. It counts nothing about how you use it.

Permissions

The app holds Android's ordinary network permission, used only for the crash reports and configuration described above. It asks for no other permission: no microphone, no notifications, no location, no contacts.

Backup

If you have Android backup switched on, Android may copy this app's data into your own Google Drive. It is your Drive and your Google account, not ours: we have no account, no server, and no way to see it.

You can turn that off in Android's own backup settings, and the app will not mind.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the shelf. Uninstalling Pontil removes everything it stored on your phone — every glass and your settings. We hold no copy anywhere. Inside the app, Settings can empty the shelf, and any one glass can be broken from its own page; both are immediate and permanent.

A copy already taken by Android backup is removed through Android's backup settings, since that copy is in your Drive and not somewhere the app can reach.

Crash reports already sent to Firebase are held under a random identifier with no account attached, expire on Google's standard schedule, and cannot be traced back to you by us.

Not medical advice

Pontil is a breathing timer. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. If breathing exercises make you dizzy or unwell, stop, and speak to a doctor before using them again.

Children

Pontil is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The crash reports described above are not tied to a person of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above changes with it. The app downloads this page when you open the policy inside it, so it shows the current text; the copy built into the app is only what it falls back to when there is no connection.
