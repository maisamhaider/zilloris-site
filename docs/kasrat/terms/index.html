Using the app

Kasrat is a workout planner for Android. You tick the machines your gym has, and it makes a month plan that uses only them, with a second choice when one is busy. Members can also share their club's machine list, so others can see a gym's machines before they join. It is free, with no ads, no purchases and no account. Installing it is the whole of it: there is nothing to register and nothing to cancel.

Kasrat is not directed at anyone under 16.

A guide, not medical advice

The plan, the weights, the rep targets and every card in the app are general suggestions worked out from simple rules and from what you entered. They are not medical, physiotherapy or coaching advice, and they do not know your health. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, are coming back from an injury or have not trained for a long time, ask a doctor before you start.

Use each machine as your gym shows you, set it to fit your body, and follow the gym's own rules. Stop if something hurts. If it keeps hurting, see a doctor or a physiotherapist. You train at your own pace and your own risk.

Deleting the app deletes the record. Android's own backup, if you have it switched on, may keep a copy in your Google account, as the privacy policy explains.

Club lists

Club lists show what members say a club has. They can be wrong or out of date: a machine may have moved, broken or gone. Check at the gym before you rely on one. Your own list on your phone always wins, and nothing on a club list changes it unless you choose "Use this list".

If you share, you agree to the rules for shared lists shown before your first share:

  • Only real machines, at real clubs. No made-up clubs, and never a home.
  • No rude words and no adverts.
  • What you share is published under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0): anyone may use it, with credit, and must share what they build from it under the same licence. You confirm you may share it: it is what you saw yourself.

We check reports within 7 days. We may remove or hide a change that breaks these rules, and stop a contributor number from writing; when we remove your change, the app tells you why. You can turn sharing off for a club and delete everything you shared at any time (Settings, Delete my shared data).

Machine lists are always free to view, never sold, and no gym ever pays to change them or their order. Clubs are sorted by distance only; not every club is listed. Club names and positions come from OpenStreetMap.
